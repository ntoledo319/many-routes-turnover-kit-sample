Turnover Kit — free sample sheets

These are the four sheets the kit prints for Larkspur Cottage, a fictional two-bedroom rental (checkout 10:00, check-in 16:00, 30-minute buffer). Every name, number and address is invented. Download, print and share them unchanged for free.

How the time plan was made

Usable minutes = check-in − checkout − buffer (16:00 − 10:00 − 30 = 330). They are split across the blocks by weight in 5-minute steps: beds 85 · bathrooms 70 · kitchen 80 · living 40 · final walk 55. Change the window and every block changes with it — that part needs the generator.

The generator

Turnover Kit is a single offline HTML file: enter your own property once, save it as a file, print these four sheets for every turnover. It is sold separately and is not in this repository. Purchase link: not yet published.
