Turnover Kit — free sample sheets licence (2026)

The sample files in this folder (sheets.html, index.html, the eight *.pdf sample sheets,
and the videos, posters, captions and transcripts in walkthroughs/) show the four printed sheets
Turnover Kit generates for "Larkspur Cottage", a fictional two-bedroom property. Every name, phone number,
address and quantity is invented.
